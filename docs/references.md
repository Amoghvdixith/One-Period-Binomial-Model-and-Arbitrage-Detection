## Foundational references

I checked the bibliographic details below against publisher and index records before citing them.

- Cox, J. C., Ross, S. A., & Rubinstein, M. (1979). Option pricing: A simplified approach. *Journal of Financial Economics*, 7(3), 229–263. https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-405X(79)90015-1
- Shreve, S. E. (2004). *Stochastic Calculus for Finance I: The Binomial Asset Pricing Model*. Springer Finance. New York: Springer. ISBN 978-0-387-40100-3. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-22527-2

Of these two references, the one-period model, the no-arbitrage condition `d < 1 + r < u`, risk-neutral pricing and replication are developed in Shreve, Chapter 1. Cox, Ross & Rubinstein (1979) is the classic paper on the binomial option-pricing approach and its multi-period extension.
